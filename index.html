Godson Iwhente is a Nigerian multidisciplinary professional whose experience spans digital marketing, social media, data analysis, media, communications and business operations.

His public professional profile describes him as a Digital Strategy Coach for African brands, with a focus on helping businesses move from simply attracting followers toward building audiences and customers.

His background in Statistics adds an analytical dimension to his marketing work, allowing him to approach communication, campaigns and business decisions with both creativity and data.

His professional journey has included work with organisations in real estate, television/media, healthcare and other business environments.
